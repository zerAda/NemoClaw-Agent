## Critical Gaps (Blockers)

### 1. Scoring Pipeline Broken at Runtime — `app.py` Signature Mismatch

**Affects:** SCORE-01, SCORE-02, SCORE-03, SCORE-04

`app.py` calls `hunter.score_job(jd_text, job.url)` passing two strings. Phase 3 rewrote `HunterService.score_job()` to accept a single `JobListing` object. At runtime every job produces an `AttributeError`. No scoring, no threshold filtering, no ScoreRecord persistence is reachable. The entire pipeline from Phase 3 onward fails silently for every job processed.

**Fix:** Update `app.py` `process_job()` to pass the `JobListing` object directly to `score_job()`.

---

### 2. No Legal Review Document — `LEGAL-01` Runtime Gate Only

**Affects:** LEGAL-01, LEGAL-03

`LEGAL_GATE_APPROVED` is an env var that gates `ApplyService`. But LEGAL-01 requires a *documented* legal review of GDPR Article 22, CNIL high-risk AI classification, and EU AI Act obligations. No such document exists in the repo. Setting the env var without the review defeats the compliance intent of Phase 8.

---

### 3. LinkedIn No Circuit Breaker — `SUBMIT-05`

`apply.py` LinkedIn Easy Apply flow has no warning-signal detection. Captchas, rate-limit pages, and account warnings will not halt submissions. This is a production safety requirement.

---

### 4. No Audit Log — `TRACK-05`

The `applications` table has no `audit_log` column or companion table. No action logging is implemented anywhere in the codebase. Requirement is entirely absent.

---

### 5. Language Detection Missing — `APPLY-01`, `APPLY-03`

`tailor.py` hardcodes `"Strict French (FR-fr)"` in the generation prompt regardless of input. English-language jobs will receive French cover letters. Phase 7's core language-detection requirement was not implemented.
